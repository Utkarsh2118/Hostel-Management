# VidyaVihar University Hostel Rules & Administration Manual (Academic Year 2026–27)

## 1. Hostel Blocks & Eligibility
- The university operates four residential blocks:
  - **Nilgiri Bhavan (Boys)** — 4 floors, located directly beside the Central Mess.
  - **Vindhya Bhavan (Boys)** — 4 floors, near the Sports Complex and Academic Wing.
  - **Kaveri Bhavan (Girls)** — 4 floors, located beside the Central Mess and Health Centre.
  - **Narmada Bhavan (Girls)** — 4 floors, quiet residential wing beside the Library Lawn.
- Strict gender-matched allotment applies: male students are eligible only for boys' blocks (Nilgiri, Vindhya) and female students only for girls' blocks (Kaveri, Narmada).
- Single-occupancy rooms are primarily prioritized for 4th-year students, subject to availability and allotment priority score.

## 2. Room Allotment Policy
- Room allotment is conducted deterministically using the HostelHub Serial Dictatorship engine.
- Priority score is computed from:
  - Academic year seniority (higher year receives higher weight).
  - Optional CGPA merit band weight, when enabled by the Chief Warden.
  - Deterministic seeded tie-breaking using the published allotment seed so any student or auditor can verify fairness.
- Students may submit up to **5 ranked room preferences** before the allotment deadline.
- Mutual roommate requests (both students listing each other) are honored whenever a feasible multi-occupancy room has sufficient open slots and both students satisfy block gender eligibility.
- Students who do not receive any of their 5 ranked choices due to capacity exhaustion are placed in the Unallotted Register with an explicit explanation and may participate in supplementary manual allotment at the Warden's Office.

## 3. Room Exchange (Swap) Rules
- Only students with an active room allotment may post a room-exchange request.
- HostelHub supports direct **2-way swaps** as well as **3-way and 4-way cycle swaps**.
- Every swap cycle must preserve block gender rules and room capacity limits.
- A swap proposal expires **72 hours** after creation if all participating students do not explicitly accept.
- No swap is executed unless **100% of participating students** click Accept. Once accepted by all parties, room reassignments execute atomically and are recorded permanently in the Hostel Audit Register.
- Unauthorized informal room exchanges without recording on HostelHub attract a fine of INR 2,000 and disciplinary warning.

## 4. Mess Timings & Dining Rules
- Central Mess operates daily for all four blocks:
  - **Breakfast**: 7:30 AM – 9:30 AM (Weekends: 8:00 AM – 10:00 AM)
  - **Lunch**: 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM
  - **Evening Tea & Snacks**: 5:00 PM – 6:15 PM
  - **Dinner**: 7:45 PM – 9:45 PM
- Mess rebate is granted only for continuous absence of **5 or more days** with prior online intimation at least 24 hours in advance.
- Carrying mess utensils, plates, or tumblers into hostel rooms is prohibited.

## 5. Gate Curfew & Night Entry
- Main hostel block gates close at **11:00 PM** for 1st-year residents and **11:30 PM** for 2nd-, 3rd-, and 4th-year residents.
- Late-entry permission for library study, hackathon participation, or train arrivals must be logged in the Gate Register before 9:00 PM.
- Overnight outstation leave requires guardian acknowledgment and warden approval on the Hostel Office register.

## 6. Visitor & Guest Policy
- Parents and official guardians may visit residents in the Ground Floor Common Visitor Lounge between **9:00 AM and 7:00 PM**.
- Day scholars and external guests are strictly not permitted inside residential corridors or student rooms without written permission from the Block Warden.
- Overnight stay of non-residents inside hostel rooms is strictly prohibited.

## 7. Electrical Appliances, AC & Maintenance
- AC rooms include wall-mounted split air conditioning; monthly electricity sub-meter charges are billed equally among room occupants at actual university tariff.
- High-wattage heating appliances (induction cooktops, immersion rods, room heaters, electric irons above 1000W) are prohibited in rooms for fire safety.
- Electric kettles under 800W and desk lamps are permitted.
- Maintenance complaints (plumbing, carpentry, Wi-Fi, electrical) must be logged before 2:00 PM for same-day technician dispatch; emergency water or power issues are attended 24x7 by the Estate Office (Ext. 4100).

## 8. Quiet Hours & Discipline
- Quiet hours are enforced from **10:30 PM to 6:30 AM** across all corridors, especially in rooms marked `quiet`.
- Smoking, alcohol, and banned substances are strictly prohibited anywhere inside hostel premises and carry immediate suspension of hostel residency.
- Residents are responsible for room furniture issued on the Room Key Tag Slip; damage beyond normal wear is recovered from the caution deposit.

## 9. Warden Office Hours & Escalation
- Block Warden Office Hours: **Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM** and **4:30 PM – 6:30 PM** (Ground Floor, Room G-01 of each Bhavan).
- Chief Warden Office: Administrative Annexe, Room 104, **3:00 PM – 5:30 PM** (Monday to Friday).
- For any policy exception, medical room-change request, or dispute not covered in this handbook, contact your Block Warden directly.
